Privacy Policy

App: periodic-assignment (3D Net Worth Table)
Last updated: November 2026

This is a demonstration web app built as a coding assignment. This policy explains what information the app uses.

Information we access

The app uses Google Sign-In only to let you log in. It requests basic sign-in permissions (your name, email address and profile picture, via the openid, email and profile scopes). The app does not read your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

How information is used

Sign-in is used only to unlock the page. The app does not display, store, share or sell your Google account information. There is no server or database behind this app, so nothing about you is saved by us.

Data shown in the app

The 3D table displays sample data loaded from a publicly shared Google Sheet. It is not personal data about you.

Cookies and storage

The app itself sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. Google may use its own cookies as part of Google Sign-In, which are governed by Google's privacy policy.

Third parties

The app relies on Google (sign-in and Google Sheets API) and loads code libraries from public CDNs. We do not share your information with any other third party.
